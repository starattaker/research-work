\begin{abstract}
We report replication progress for alveolar bone-loss \emph{severity} on DenPAR: YOLOv8x tooth detection, three Keypoint R-CNN heads (CEJ, intersection, apex), and preprocessing ablations v1--v4. Region-growing label assignment (v4, 1--8\,px rings) yields the best keypoint test OKS (CEJ 0.921, intersection 0.822, apex 0.853 vs.\ paper 0.954/0.912/0.815). End-to-end severity ICC evaluation is the remaining stage. Pattern classification and segmentation are out of scope.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The target publication combines tooth detection, anatomical keypoints, and severity scoring. Our replication restricts scope to detection, keypoints, and intraclass correlation (ICC) against expert severity labels. DenPAR provides boxes, CEJ/apex clicks, bone lines, and tooth masks; \emph{intersection keypoints} and single/double root labels are derived by us.

\section{Dataset and Preprocessing Ablation}
DenPAR contains 1{,}000 images (650/150/200 train/val/test) and 4{,}402 annotated teeth. Bounding boxes are unchanged across ablations; assignment rules for apex/CEJ differ. Tie-break for mask-based strategies uses the **segmentation centroid**, not the bbox center.

\subsection{Region growing (v4)}
Expert CEJ/apex clicks are assigned in passes: (0) on the tooth mask; (1--8) unassigned clicks in outward distance bands of 1\,px up to 8\,px from the mask; beyond 8\,px the click is dropped. Ties between teeth use the nearest mask centroid. This replaces v3's single 4\,px grace constant with a principled ring expansion (Fig.~\ref{fig:region-grow}).

\textbf{Reading the ablation:} v3's fixed 4\,px grace retains CEJ well but **drops 23.7\% of apex clicks** and inflates 0-apex teeth (37.7\%). v4 (region growing to 8\,px) improves apex retention (9.0\% drop) while keeping CEJ (0.6\% drop). **Test OKS after training** (Table~\ref{tab:kpt-all}) confirms v4 as the best preprocessing variant; apex OKS **exceeds** the paper target.

\section{Tooth Detection (YOLOv8x)}
Training followed paper hyperparameters (Adam, lr $10^{-4}$, cosine schedule, 200 epochs max, patience 25). On a Pop!\_OS workstation (RTX $\sim$12\,GB), batch size 4 was used.

\section{Keypoint R-CNN}
Three ResNet-50-FPN Keypoint R-CNN models (2 keypoints per tooth, CLAHE augmentation) were trained per preprocessing ablation on a collaborator RTX $\sim$12\,GB GPU (batch 4, patience 30).

v1 CEJ OKS was 0.820. **v4 is selected** for the final inference pipeline. Apex is the only head that exceeds the paper OKS on test; CEJ and intersection gaps remain and may reflect label derivation (intersection) and GT-box training vs.\ paper setup.

\textbf{Training uses ground-truth boxes} from preprocessed JSON, not YOLO outputs. At inference, YOLO boxes replace GT---ICC is the true end-to-end metric.

\section{Tooling and visual QA}
We added TensorBoard logging, loss-curve plots, and standalone visual testers for YOLO and Keypoint R-CNN. Preprocessing comparison is automated via \texttt{scripts/compare\_preprocessing.py}. Grace-band QA (\texttt{visualize\_v3\_v4\_grace.py}) and region-growing diagrams (\texttt{draw\_region\_growing\_diagram.py}) produce paper figures and animation frames (PNG sequence + GIF) under \texttt{research\_log/figures/} and \texttt{paper/figures/}.

\section{Experiment Tracking}
Metrics are recorded automatically at the end of each \texttt{src.keypoint.train} run (no manual collect step). Append-only records live under \texttt{research\_log/experiments/records/}; the latest per experiment is indexed in \texttt{registry.json} and summarized in \texttt{paper\_table.json} for the paper table. When all three keypoint models finish (CEJ, intersection, apex), the pipeline updates \texttt{CHECKPOINT.md}, \texttt{06\_keypoint\_training.md}, and \texttt{paper/generated\_keypoint\_table.tex}, then commits and pushes log files.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Processed data: \texttt{data/processed\_v\{1,2,3,4\}/}
  \item Weights: \texttt{runs/keypoints/v\{2,3,4\}\_\{cej,intersection,apex\}/best.pt}
  \item Runners: \texttt{run\_experiment\_v2.sh}, \texttt{v3.sh}, \texttt{v4.sh}
\end{itemize}
Reusing the same \texttt{--output-dir} overwrites \texttt{best.pt} when validation improves; historical metrics remain in timestamped registry records.
