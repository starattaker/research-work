\begin{abstract}
We report incremental progress replicating a panoramic radiograph pipeline for alveolar bone-loss \emph{severity} estimation: YOLOv8x tooth detection, three Keypoint R-CNN models (CEJ, bone-line intersection, apex), and planned ICC evaluation. We document preprocessing ablations (8\,px bbox margin vs.\ strict bbox vs.\ mask-based cleaning), hardware-limited training on a 12\,GB GPU, and visual QA tooling. Pattern classification and segmentation are out of scope.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The target publication combines tooth detection, anatomical keypoints, and severity scoring. Our replication restricts scope to detection, keypoints, and intraclass correlation (ICC) against expert severity labels. DenPAR provides boxes, CEJ/apex clicks, bone lines, and tooth masks; \emph{intersection keypoints} and single/double root labels are derived by us.

\section{Dataset and Preprocessing Ablation}
DenPAR contains 1{,}000 images (650/150/200 train/val/test) and 4{,}402 annotated teeth. Bounding boxes are unchanged across ablations; assignment rules for apex/CEJ differ. Tie-break for mask-based strategies uses the **segmentation centroid**, not the bbox center.

\textbf{Reading the ablation:} v3's fixed 4\,px grace retains CEJ well but **drops 23.7\% of apex clicks** and inflates 0-apex teeth (37.7\%), shifting single/double labels. v4 (region growing to 8\,px) improves apex retention (9.0\% drop) while keeping CEJ (0.6\% drop). Label statistics alone do not prove clinical correctness---**test OKS after training** is the final judge (v2 CEJ OKS 0.843 vs.\ v1 0.820).

\section{Tooth Detection (YOLOv8x)}
Training followed paper hyperparameters (Adam, lr $10^{-4}$, cosine schedule, 200 epochs max, patience 25). On a Pop!\_OS workstation (RTX $\sim$12\,GB), batch size 4 was used.

\section{Keypoint R-CNN}
Three ResNet-50-FPN Keypoint R-CNN models (2 keypoints per tooth, CLAHE augmentation) per preprocessing ablation. **v2 (strict bbox)** completed on a collaborator RTX $\sim$12\,GB GPU:

v1 CEJ OKS was 0.820. v3/v4 labels are rebuilt; keypoint training pending.

\textbf{Training uses ground-truth boxes} from preprocessed JSON, not YOLO outputs. Visual overlays may show many red boxes because raw inference emits $\sim$30 proposals per image; we filter with score threshold 0.5 and NMS IoU 0.6 for QA (paper inference setting).

\section{Experiment Tracking}
Metrics are recorded automatically at the end of each \texttt{src.keypoint.train} run (no manual collect step). Append-only records live under \texttt{research\_log/experiments/records/}; the latest per experiment is indexed in \texttt{registry.json} and summarized in \texttt{paper\_table.json} for the paper table. When all three keypoint models finish (CEJ, intersection, apex), the pipeline updates \texttt{CHECKPOINT.md}, \texttt{06\_keypoint\_training.md}, and \texttt{paper/generated\_keypoint\_table.tex}, then commits and pushes log files.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Processed data: \texttt{data/processed\_v\{1,2,3,4\}/}
  \item Weights: \texttt{runs/keypoints/v\{2,3,4\}\_\{cej,intersection,apex\}/best.pt}
  \item Runners: \texttt{run\_experiment\_v2.sh}, \texttt{v3.sh}, \texttt{v4.sh}
\end{itemize}
Reusing the same \texttt{--output-dir} overwrites \texttt{best.pt} when validation improves; historical metrics remain in timestamped registry records.

\section{Tooling}
We added TensorBoard logging, loss-curve plots, and standalone visual testers for YOLO and Keypoint R-CNN. Preprocessing comparison is automated via \texttt{scripts/compare\_preprocessing.py}.
